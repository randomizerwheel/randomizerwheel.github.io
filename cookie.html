Cookie Policy

Transparent disclosure regarding how we use cookies and browser storage technologies to keep your experience seamless.

Last Updated: May 28, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device by your web browser when you visit certain websites. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, improve performance, and provide data analytics to site owners.

In addition to traditional cookies, RandomizerWheel utilizes modern HTML5 web technologies such as Local Storage and Session Storage to deliver core application features without requiring a remote database or server account registration.

2. How We Use Cookies and Local Storage

We implement these data storage technologies to fulfill essential app behaviors, enhance visual customization choices, and manage advertisement configurations. They are categorized as follows:

Storage Type Purpose Data Tracked
Essential Local Storage Saves your customized spin text, colors, active tabs, and results logs natively on your device so your data is not lost when refreshing. Custom text entries, preset lists, results history logs.
Functional Preferences Remembers your audio toggle status, chosen visual color theme (e.g., Neon, Rainbow, Fire), and confetti explosion triggers. Sound options, chosen theme name, animation toggles.
Advertising & Analytics Deployed by third-party vendor platforms to display personalized commercial elements and evaluate aggregated user interaction spikes. Anonymized browser fingerprints, dynamic page clicks, geolocational sources.

3. Third-Party Cookie Providers

Because RandomizerWheel is entirely free to use, we collaborate with external optimization networks and marketing agencies to fund our operating costs and server maintenance. These integrated frameworks include:

  • Google AdSense: Google utilizes DART cookies to customize relevant ad delivery pathways mapped directly to your historical internet footprint. Users can manage or permanently deactivate personalized ad settings by visiting the Google Ads Settings interface.
  • Analytics Scripts: Basic, privacy-compliant tracking mechanisms observe non-identifiable web telemetry records (such as device types, loading durations, and exit locations) to help optimize application page performance across regional scaling zones.

4. Managing and Controlling Cookies

You have full autonomy over how cookies and localized browser storage files function on your terminal device:

  • Wiping Local Storage Content: You can completely clear your user choices, presets, and entries at any time by clicking the "🗑 Clear All" or "Clear History" button located directly inside the wheel application interface controls panel.
  • Browser Settings Adjustments: You can configure your browser to block cookies entirely, notify you when a new cookie is set, or automatically clear local storage files upon closing your browsing instance. Check your browser's "Help" documentation (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) to find step-by-step directions.
