Nothing kills trust in a live giveaway faster than a winner picked "off-screen." Viewers want to watch the moment happen in real time. A random wheel spinner shown directly on your stream gives every entrant a visible, provably fair shot — and doubles as genuinely entertaining content. Here's how creators use RandomizerWheel to run transparent giveaways on TikTok Live, Instagram Live, and Twitch.

Why an On-Screen Wheel Builds Trust

Viewers are naturally skeptical of giveaways — bots, insider picks, and rigged results are common complaints across social platforms. Spinning a random wheel picker live, where the audience can literally watch the pointer land, removes that doubt instantly. It also creates a genuine moment of suspense that keeps viewers watching until the very end instead of dropping off before the reveal.

Setting Up Your Giveaway Wheel for a Stream

Collect entries however your platform allows — comments, a hashtag, or a follow-to-enter form — then paste the list of usernames into the custom spin wheel. Switch on Remove Winner if you're giving away more than one prize, so the same person can't win twice in the same stream.

Share your screen (or use a browser source in OBS/Streamlabs) so the wheel is visible to your audience the entire time you're adding names. This visible entry process is what separates a trustworthy giveaway from a suspicious one.

Adding the Wheel as an OBS Browser Source

In OBS or Streamlabs, add a new Browser Source and point it at your saved wheel link (generated with the Copy Link button). Set the resolution to match your canvas and position it as an overlay or full-screen scene for the giveaway moment. Because the wheel runs entirely in-browser, no plugins or extensions are required.

Giveaway Best Practices

A few habits make your giveaways look more professional and keep your community happy long term:

  • Announce entry rules clearly before you start collecting names, and stick to them.
  • Double-check your entry list for duplicate usernames before spinning.
  • Screen-record or clip the spin moment so you can repost proof of the winner afterward.
  • Use Remove Winner for multi-prize giveaways so odds stay fair for every remaining entrant.
  • Thank every entrant, not just the winner — it keeps people coming back for the next giveaway.
