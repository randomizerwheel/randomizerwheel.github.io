"What do you want to play?" shouldn't take longer than the game itself. A custom spin wheel turns decision-making into part of the fun — instead of debating for ten minutes, you spin and go. Here are 15 tested wheel ideas that turn any ordinary evening into a proper family game night, all buildable in the free RandomizerWheel spinner in under a minute.

Classic Decision Wheels

Start simple with wheels that solve the eternal "what should we do" debate.

  • Movie Genre Wheel: Comedy, action, animated, mystery, documentary — spin to pick the mood for movie night.
  • Board Game Wheel: List every board game in your closet and let the wheel end the negotiation.
  • Restaurant Wheel: For family takeout night, load in your five go-to spots and spin instead of scrolling delivery apps for 20 minutes.
  • Who Goes First Wheel: Add every player's name so no one can claim they always go last.

Active & Party Game Wheels

These wheels turn the spin itself into the activity.

  • Truth or Dare Wheel: Split the wheel into truth prompts and dare challenges for a party-ready twist.
  • Charades Category Wheel: Animals, movies, jobs, actions — spin for a category before each round.
  • Dance Move Wheel: Add silly dance moves for a musical-chairs-style party game with younger kids.
  • Would You Rather Wheel: Pre-load a set of "would you rather" prompts for road trips or dinner table games.

Creative Challenge Wheels

Great for family talent shows, rainy afternoons, or screen-free time.

  • Drawing Prompt Wheel: Animal, object, superhero, food — spin and sketch in 60 seconds.
  • Story Starter Wheel: Load in random opening lines and take turns building a story around them.
  • Talent Show Wheel: Sing, dance, joke, impression — spin to decide each performer's category.
  • Snack Wheel: Movie night snack roulette — popcorn, chips, ice cream, fruit.

Team & Scoring Wheels

Add structure to competitive family game nights.

  • Team Picker Wheel: Randomly split players into two fair teams for trivia or relay games.
  • Points Wheel: Assign random bonus points (5, 10, 15, 20) to keep trivia night unpredictable.
  • Punishment Wheel: Light, silly consequences for the losing team — sing a song, do 10 jumping jacks, tell a joke.
